# Open web pages in the browser

Links to web pages open in your browser, while images, galleries and videos keep opening in BaconReader.

- A link to a web page, such as a news article, opens in your browser, not in BaconReader's own browser.
- Tapping the title of an open post opens a link to a web page in your browser.
- Links to Reddit, and to images, GIFs, galleries and videos that BaconReader shows itself, open in BaconReader as before.

**Settings** (BaconReader → Settings → Patches, under "Links"):
- **Open web pages in the browser** (off by default): links to web pages open in your browser. Images, GIFs, galleries
  and videos still open in BaconReader, whether or not "Open links inside app" is on.

**Limits**:
- Links to Imgur, RedGIFs and Reddit always open in BaconReader, even when they lead to a web page.
