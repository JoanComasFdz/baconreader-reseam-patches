# Full screen post videos

A full screen button on a post's video, as on videos in comments.

- While the controls of a post's video show, a full screen button shows in its top right corner. It shows the video
  over the whole screen, turned sideways when it's wider than high. Back or the button closes the full screen, and the
  video plays on in the post.
- Videos in the full-screen viewer and the browser don't get the button.

**Settings** (BaconReader → Settings → Patches, under "Images and videos"):
- **Full screen button** (on by default): shows the full screen button over a post's video.

**Limits**:
- BaconReader shows a post's media in its detail view only with its own "Show large image" on (Settings → Detail
  View).
